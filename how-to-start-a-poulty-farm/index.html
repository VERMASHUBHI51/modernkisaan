How to start a Poulty Farm

Shubhi 3 February 2026 Animals

Poultry farming is one of India’s fastest-growing agribusiness sectors. India ranks among the top global producers of eggs and chicken meat. If you’re considering starting a poultry farm in India, this complete guide covers planning, investment and risk management.

Why Start a Poultry Farming Business in India?

Poultry farming offers several advantages:

1. Strong Market Demand
Eggs and chicken are affordable protein sources and are consumed across urban and rural India.

2. Quick Production Cycles
Broiler chickens reach market weight in 35–42 days, allowing multiple cycles per year.

3. Scalability
You can start small (500–1,000 birds) and gradually expand.

4. Government Support
Various NABARD schemes and agricultural loans support poultry entrepreneurs.

5. Employment Generation
The sector creates jobs in rural and semi-urban regions.

However, profitability depends on management efficiency — not just demand.

Guide to Starting a Poultry Farm

Select a model based on your budget and goals.

1. Broiler Farming

  • Meat production
  • 5–6 week cycle
  • Faster returns
  • Higher price fluctuation risk

2. Layer Farming

  • Egg production
  • Long-term steady income
  • Requires longer commitment (72+ weeks cycle)

3. Contract Farming

  • Integrator provides chicks, feed, medicine
  • Lower risk
  • Fixed growing charges
  • Lower profit margin

4. Hatchery or Integrated Farming

  • Higher investment
  • More technical knowledge required

For beginners, broiler or contract farming is usually recommended.

Conduct Market Research

  • Identify local buyers (traders, hotels, processors)
  • Study seasonal price trends
  • Analyze feed cost in your area
  • Check distance to nearest poultry processing plant
  • Understand diseases in your region

Poultry farming is highly price-sensitive. Entering without market clarity can result in losses.

Prepare a Detailed Business Plan

A proper poultry business plan should include:

  • Farm capacity (1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 birds)
  • Land and shed cost
  • Equipment cost
  • Feed cost (60–70% of total cost)
  • Mortality assumption (3–7%)
  • Break-even analysis
  • Risk mitigation strategy

A well-documented plan is essential if you’re applying for a bank loan.

Select the Right Location

An ideal poultry farm location should have:

  • Proper drainage (no flooding)
  • Reliable electricity
  • Clean water supply
  • Road accessibility
  • Safe distance from residential areas

In hot climates, build sheds in the east-west direction to reduce heat stress. Heat stress significantly affects bird growth and mortality.

Build Proper Infrastructure

Your poultry shed must ensure:

  • Good ventilation
  • Adequate spacing (1 sq ft per broiler bird approx.)
  • Predator protection
  • Insulation for temperature control
  • Easy cleaning and disinfection

Procure High-Quality Chicks

Always buy from certified hatcheries.

Healthy chicks should:

  • Be active and uniform in size
  • Have proper vaccination records
  • Show no deformities

Low-quality chicks increase mortality and reduce feed conversion efficiency.

Implement Strong Biosecurity

  1. Restrict visitor entry
  2. Install footbaths
  3. Maintain vaccination schedule
  4. Regular disinfection
  5. Rodent and pest control
  6. Separate clothing for workers

Prevention is cheaper than treatment.

Market weight target: 2 - 2.5 kg in 35 - 42 days.

Is Poultry Farming Profitable in India?

Yes, if managed efficiently.

A 1,000-bird broiler farm can generate a margin of ₹8–₹20 per bird in stable markets.

However, profits depend on:

  1. Feed prices
  2. Mortality rate
  3. Market demand
  4. Operational efficiency

Feed alone accounts for nearly 70% of production cost. Efficient feed management determines success.

Government Schemes and Loans

You may explore:

  • NABARD Poultry Venture Capital schemes
  • Agricultural term loans
  • Mudra loans for small farmers
  • State-level animal husbandry subsidies

Tips for Long-Term Success

Long-term success in any livestock or farming venture requires patience and planning. Start small and scale gradually so you can manage risks and learn from experience. Focus on building strong relationships with buyers to ensure consistent demand and better pricing. Maintain strict hygiene standards to keep your stock healthy and reduce losses. If feasible, diversify into both eggs and meat to create multiple income streams. Most importantly, keep accurate financial records to track costs, profits, and areas for improvement.

If you approach poultry farming like a structured business it can become a reliable and sustainable income source.
